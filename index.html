Universal Translator

Interdisciplinary Frame Translation

Experiment Viewer ArXiv Translations

Can knowledge from one academic field be systematically translated into another? This project reframes graduate-level exam questions from one subfield into the conceptual vocabulary of a second field, then tests whether a domain expert in that second field can answer them — without any knowledge of the original field.

Explore experiments →

How it works

1
BaselineA target-field expert answers the original, untranslated question — establishing a baseline.
2
TranslateThe question is reframed into the target subfield's vocabulary by a translator model.
3
TranslatedThe same target-field expert answers the translated question.
4
Back-translateThe translated question is mapped back into source-field terms.
5
FaithfulnessA source-field expert answers the back-translated question — a proxy for how much meaning was preserved.
